# Welcome to Research Observer

This page exists only as a starter. Replace it with your own `00_*.md`, add `01_*.md`, `02_*.md`, and continue in whatever sequence fits your research.

## What belongs here?

Use progress notes for questions, hypotheses, reading synthesis, experiment plans, implementation decisions, results, failures, figures, or next actions. The observer treats Markdown as the source of truth instead of forcing a fixed thesis structure.

## Equations work

Inline math such as $p(y\mid x)$ and display math render with KaTeX:

$$
\hat{\theta} = \arg\min_{\theta} \mathcal{L}(\theta)
$$

## Figures stay close to the research

Create `progress/figures/`, put an asset there, then reference it normally:

```md
![Experiment result](figures/result.svg)
```

You can use SVG/PNG/JPG/WebP/GIF/AVIF, PDF, MP4/WebM, and common audio files.
