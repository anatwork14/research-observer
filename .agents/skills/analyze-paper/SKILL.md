---
name: analyze-paper
description: Analyze a local research PDF and connect its evidence to Research Observer notes. Use when summarizing a paper, extracting claims, comparing a paper with the workspace, or drafting a literature note. Do not invent bibliographic metadata or page citations.
---

1. Read `AGENTS.md` and `progress/AGENTS.md`.
2. Identify the exact PDF under `progress/` and any companion literature note.
3. Work from the PDF's extracted text/page context or user-selected text. Do not claim to have read pages that were not available.
4. Capture page numbers for direct evidence whenever possible.
5. Separate:
   - bibliographic facts,
   - author claims,
   - reported evidence/results,
   - limitations,
   - your synthesis relative to this workspace.
6. Search existing notes for related questions, experiments, results, and decisions before proposing a new note.
7. If drafting a literature note, use verified `pdf`, `authors`, `year`, and `doi` fields only.
8. Do not convert a paper's claim into a workspace conclusion without explicitly stating the inference.
9. End with concrete relationships to existing research and unresolved questions.
10. Run `npm run doctor` after any file edit.
